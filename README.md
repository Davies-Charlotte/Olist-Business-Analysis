# Olist Marketplace Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the performance of Olist, a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace, using SQL and Power BI.

The objective was to understand the key drivers of marketplace performance, identify operational challenges, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve revenue growth, customer satisfaction and delivery performance.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis explores:

### Sales Performance
- How has revenue and order volume changed over time?
- Is marketplace growth driven by customer spending or transaction volume?

### Product Performance
- Which product categories drive the most orders and revenue?
- Is revenue concentrated among a small number of categories?

### Regional Performance
- Which states and cities contribute the most revenue?
- Where are the strongest opportunities for growth?

### Seller Performance
- Are marketplace revenues concentrated among a small number of sellers?
- Which sellers perform best operationally?

### Delivery Performance
- How reliable is marketplace delivery?
- Which routes and regions contribute most to delays?

### Customer Experience
- How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?

---

## Tools Used

- SQL (DuckDB)
- Power BI

---

## Key Insights

- Marketplace growth was primarily driven by increased order volume rather than higher average order value.
- Revenue was concentrated among a small number of product categories.
- São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais represented the largest customer markets.
- Delivery delays had a significant negative impact on customer satisfaction.
- Operational improvements on high-volume routes would likely create the greatest marketplace impact.
